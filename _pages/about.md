I'm an early career mathematician working and teaching in Sydney, NSW. My mathematical interests lie in non-commutative harmonic analysis and distribution theory.

I completed my Honours degree at the [University of Technology Sydney](https://www.uts.edu.au/) in mid-2026 under the supervision of Tony Dooley, with a [thesis on the representation theory of Lie groups and the orbit method](/publications/2026-01-06-kirillov-orbit-method/). Since then I have been teaching and researching at UTS, and I hope to begin a PhD in mid-2027.

My recent work with David Lee includes a [Liouville theorem for Lévy operators on nilpotent and compact Lie groups](/publications/2026-11-14-liouville-levy-operators-lie-groups/) and a proof that [the fundamental solution of the logarithmic Laplacian is a function in dimensions 1 and 2](/publications/2026-11-22-fundamental-solution-logarithmic-laplacian/).
