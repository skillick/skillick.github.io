Honours thesis submitted for the degree of Bachelor of Mathematical Sciences (Honours) at the University of Technology Sydney, supervised by Anthony Dooley.

**Summary.** An introduction to the representation theory of Lie groups through Kirillov's orbit method. Starting from Lie groups and Lie algebras, the thesis develops coadjoint orbits and their canonical symplectic form, then treats the unitary dual of nilpotent Lie groups (working through the Heisenberg group in detail) and the correspondence between coadjoint orbits and irreducible representations of compact Lie groups, including the character formula. It closes with geometric quantisation and the example of $$SU(2)$$.
