**Authors:** Samuel Killick and David Lee

**Abstract.** Let $$\mathcal{L}$$ be a left-invariant Lévy operator, with characteristics $$(a, b, \mu)$$, on a connected and simply connected Lie group $$G$$ that is either compact or nilpotent with $$[G,[G,G]]$$ relatively compact (equivalently, of step at most two). We prove that every bounded distributional solution of $$\mathcal{L}u = 0$$ is right-invariant under the closed subgroup $$P = \overline{\langle \exp \mathfrak{h}_0 \cup \operatorname{supp} \mu \rangle}$$, where $$\mathfrak{h}_0 = \operatorname{Lie}\langle b, \operatorname{ran}(a) \rangle$$, and is constant if and only if $$P = G$$. In the compact case we give three proofs, one through the Itô–Kawada equidistribution theorem, one by propagation of the maximum and one by an application of the Peter–Weyl theorem, while the nilpotent case rests on the Choquet–Deny theorem of Bénard and Frisch.
